Collections 
    tickets: title, description, category, status #, assigned_to, resolved_by 
    users: username, password, role 

Task
    Employee Janaki raising the complaint to the service team 'My Laptop is not on'.
    TL Khushi assigns the ticket raised by Janaki Support Engineer Ganavi.
    Admin will create Janaki, Kushi, anb Ganavi users. 

Solution
1. Create mongo database 'it_service_desk_db'
use it_service_desk_db
2. Add four users Admin, Janaki, Kushi, anb Ganavi
db.users.insertMany( [
    {username: "Admin", password: "admin123", role:"admin"},
    {username: "Janaki", password: "janaki123", role:"employee"},
    {username: "JanKushiaki", password: "kushi123", role:"team_lead"},
    {username: "Ganavi", password: "ganavi123", role:"support_eng"}
])
db.users.find()
3. Janaki will create the ticket 
db.tickets.insertOne({title:'laptop is not on', description: 'the power button is not working',
  category:'Hardware', status:'NEW'})
db.tickets.find()
4. Khushi assigns the ticket to Ganavi
db.tickets.updateOne({_id:ObjectId("???")}, {$set:{status:"ASSIGNED"}})
db.tickets.find()
5. Ganavi will update the issue resolution status 
db.tickets.updateOne({_id:ObjectId("???")}, {$set:{status:"RESOLVED"}})
db.tickets.find()
