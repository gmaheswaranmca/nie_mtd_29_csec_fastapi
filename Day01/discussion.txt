To add the 'student' document to students collection:
$ db.students.insertOne({name:"Anvesh", age:20, course:"CSE", year:2}) 

To list students documents:
$ db.students.find()

$ db.students.insertOne({name:"Gaurangi", age:20, course:"ISE", year:2})

$ db.students.find()

$ db.students.insertMany([
    {name:"Kunal", age:20, course:"CSE", year:2},
    {name:"Manoj", age:20, course:"CSE", year:2} 
   ])

$ db.students.find()

To query document by id: 
$ db.students.findOne({_id: ObjectId('6ab0ddc09a70a5683c3682d4')})

To filter / query by name: 
$ db.students.findOne({name: "Anvesh"})

To filter by course "CSE":
$ db.students.find({course: "CSE"})

To filter by course "ISE":
$ db.students.find({course: "ISE"})

To update Anvesh age as 21:
$ db.students.updateOne({name:"Anvesh"},{$set:{age:21}})

To delete Mahesh after adding to students collection:
db.students.insertOne({name:"Mahesh", age:47, course:'CSE', year:2})

db.students.find()

db.students.deleteOne({name:"Mahesh"})
